## Privacy Policy

This website (andreaazzola.com) uses **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to collect anonymized information about visits and usage patterns. The purpose is to understand how visitors interact with the site and to improve the content offered.

### Data Collected
- Pages visited and time spent on the site  
- Browser, operating system, and device type (aggregated and anonymized)  
- Approximate geographic area (no precise location data)  

**IP anonymization** is enabled by default in GA4, so personal IP addresses are not stored in full.

### Use of Cookies
Google Analytics sets cookies to enable tracking of visits. These cookies are only activated after the visitor has given explicit consent via the cookie banner.

### Third Parties
The data collected are processed by Google Ireland Limited. For more details, please refer to [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### How to Opt-Out
Visitors can:  
- Deny consent through the cookie banner (no tracking will occur)  
- Delete or block cookies in their browser settings  
- Use the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
